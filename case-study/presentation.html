PART 2 · SCORING & TIERING Slide 03 / 06

Two scores. One 2×2 matrix. Four tiers.

Framework: Fit × Intent. Intent (weekly) measures buying-window opening. Fit (monthly) measures deal economics, proxy for ACV potential.

Intent Score · weekly · /100
intent = Σ signal_weights + bonus

Multi-signal bonus: 2 sig → +5 · 3 sig → +12 · 4+ → +25
